    <img src="icon/gif.gif" alt="Preview">
</p>

Tired of manually moving armors to inventory and putting each of them on the armor stand?

This is a simple mod that adds 1 feature -

**Shift right click an armor stand to swap your whole armor set**

**Set a keybind to only trigger on key press**

Stop wasting time clearing hotbar slots just to swap your armor!

Supports Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Quilt, 1.16.5 ~ 1.21

Requires [Architectury API](https://modrinth.com/mod/architectury-api)

Required on client and server
